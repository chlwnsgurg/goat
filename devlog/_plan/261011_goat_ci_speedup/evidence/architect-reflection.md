That adds real child-process work inside the 0.1 s stop loops (`packages/ssh/src/tunnel.ts:1145-1151,1337-1341`) and can create an artificial >=20-iteration result. Separate loop invocations by their `WAIT_COUNT=0` resets, and avoid treating a trace-only 20-count as proof of ordinary timing behavior. No other material gap remains in the rechecked sections.
